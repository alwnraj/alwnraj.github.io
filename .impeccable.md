## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, research collaborators, and peers reviewing Alwin Rajkumar's personal portfolio. They need to quickly understand the scope, technical substance, and evidence behind projects.

### Brand Personality
Thoughtful, capable, and personal. The portfolio should feel calm and editorial at its core, while making room for technically credible research demonstrations when the work warrants it.

### Aesthetic Direction
Maintain the existing restrained serif-led portfolio style. For research work, test a complementary lab-notebook direction that prioritizes readable evidence, concrete outcomes, and live demos without overwhelming the overall site.

### Design Principles
- Let demonstrated work, especially video and measured outcomes, carry the visual weight.
- Keep copy concise, specific, and honest about the degree of autonomy.
- Preserve the portfolio's narrow, readable layout and calm pacing.
- Make experiments and their evidence scannable for technical reviewers.
- Support keyboard, mobile, reduced-motion, and high-contrast-friendly use by default.
